VR Moving Room

HLSC 4025 · A virtual version of Lee & Aronson’s (1974) moving room for the Meta Quest. The walls and ceiling of a dotted room move slowly toward and away from the participant while the floor stays still. The headset records how the participant’s head sways, so you can see whether the body follows the room.

1 · Settings

Defaults: 0.20 Hz (one cycle every 5 s), 15 cm, 60 s. Frequency and amplitude can also be changed from the controller while in VR.

2 · Run it

Before entering VR: set the headset’s boundary to stationary, have a spotter stand behind the participant, and keep a chair nearby. Stop right away if the participant feels dizzy or sick.

Controller buttons (either controller, held by the experimenter)

TriggerStart a trial (3-s countdown, then beep) · press again to stop early
A or XChange condition: still → moving → moving, resist → still
B or YRe-center the room in front of the participant (do this once they are standing still, facing forward)
Thumbstick up / downAmplitude ± 1 cm
Thumbstick left / rightFrequency ± 0.02 Hz
Meta button / take off headsetLeave VR and see the results on this page
